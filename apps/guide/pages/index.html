The OpenBoa family

OpenBoa
In OpenBoa, Boa stands for Business of Agents. Breaking limits is the permanent purpose; we put it to work by building the Business of Agents in the open. Write OpenBoa with a capital O and B in company introductions and credits.
Projects
Lead with the project’s own name when someone is using it. Write the repository names meatball, sourdough and yogurt in lowercase. In a product interface, use the display name that project has chosen. A food emoji can identify the project beside its name.
Agents
Kai has a sage body with a narrow top notch. Sol has a coral oval with a notch at its base. Use the name and familiar silhouette together where someone chooses or returns to an agent.

Write for the moment

A repository description can reward a second reading. Someone looking for a missing agent needs the result and a way forward. The roster says “No matching agents.” The button reads Clear filters. Name the action a person can actually take.

Logo

Upright 9

The small notch on Kai’s top edge repeats nine times around one uneven outline. It gives the company and its agents a family resemblance. The company signs with Upright 9.

Place the signature

Use the horizontal SVG as one piece. In the B2 construction, the flat capital B is 60 units high and the visible symbol is 80. The gap from the symbol’s right edge to the O’s left edge is 18 units. The symbol’s center sits 3 units below the capital’s center.

The supplied ink-blue and dark-lettered artwork reads on paper. On a dark page, a paper-colored header gives it the same ground as the example.

Type and color

One family from signature to interface

Rethink Sans gives the round O room inside a heavy wordmark. The signature uses weight 760. Set headings at 570, paragraphs at 425 and interface labels at 550. In lists of IDs or times, use tabular figures so the digits line up.
