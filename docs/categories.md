# Design ownership

A domain groups related capabilities. The area named on an intent owns its behavior. Changes are ordered only by their recorded predecessors.

## Brand expression

- **Company identity** (`identity`). Company signature, editorial attribution and the relationship to project and agent identities.
- **Language and naming** (`language`). English voice, terms and truthful interface wording.
- **Artwork and agents** (`artwork`). Individual assets, repertoire, agent silhouettes and eye expression.

## System foundations

- **Design foundations** (`foundations`). Shared values, accessible roles, type and layout.
- **Data visualization** (`data-visualization`). Presentation of authorized series, values and missing coverage.

## Product interface

- **Components** (`controls`). Accessible behavior of individual controls and their immediate compositions.
- **Product patterns** (`patterns`). Joined screen states that answer a complete task, including product-owned recovery.

## System delivery

- **Platform adapters** (`renderers`). Packages for the web and native platform, including compatibility evidence.
- **Guide and authoring** (`guidance`). Discovery, examples and versioned guidance for designers, developers and agents.
- **Interaction quality** (`quality`). Independent observations of appearance, motion and accessibility.
- **Consumer adoption** (`adoption`). Distribution, pinning, checks, upgrades and recipient feedback.
