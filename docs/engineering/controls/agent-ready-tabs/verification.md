# Tabs replacement evidence

At 320 px dark and 1440 px light, the pill and dock examples were captured before and after replacement in their initial and settled selected states. Seven pairs matched byte for byte; the other differed by four pixels, at most one channel value each. These captures do not compare every animation frame.

Installed Vite and Next.js fixtures exercised allowed and refused agent selection, disabled selection, keyboard wrapping, Home and End, selection callback counts, panel relationships and retained panel content. Invalid and all-disabled selections were exercised. The existing motion-gallery checks exercised tab and dock interaction. Native rendering, a complete motion-frame comparison and independent visual approval remain separate.
