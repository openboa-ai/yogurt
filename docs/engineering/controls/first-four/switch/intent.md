# Change which dates I see

Draft intent r1. A person reading records should be able to hide or show their dates and immediately see the consequence. The preference is independent of editing the view name.

**Success.** Pressing anywhere on the labeled switch row changes the displayed dates and the spoken checked state together. Reversing the setting mid-motion lands on the most recent choice.

**Failure worth catching.** The thumb comes to rest on the right after the user switched it off, or an unsaved name is submitted along with the preference.

The host owns the preference and whether it can apply it. Yogurt owns the labeled control, hit area and transition. [Paired spec](spec.md) · [Shared study](../README.md)
