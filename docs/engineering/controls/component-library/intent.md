# Use one OpenBoa UI library

A product author can start with a familiar button, field or menu and build through to a decision or conversation without designing its spacing, states or palette from scratch. A person moving between Sourdough and Meatball should recognize the same controls and recovery behavior.

Yogurt owns every visible role. The author picks an emphasis, density, surface and an available action. The product supplies its records, language, authority and effects.

Follow the [implementation specification](spec.md) and the [public inventory](../../../components.md).

## Agent presence

Make Kai and Sol feel attentive through the timing of their eyes and bodies. A glance leads a small turn. The original notch and color identify the agent, even in a compact roster. Presence expresses the work state the app already knows. Each state stays readable in words and with a still portrait.
