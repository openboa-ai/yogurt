## Requirements

- **I01-U01A.** Keep the Upright 9 silhouette, the outlined Rethink Sans wordmark at 760 and the B2 relation: 80 symbol units to 60 cap-height units, an 18-unit gap and a 3-unit optical center adjustment.

**Constraint.** Use the selected Upright 9, B2 proportions and outlined 760 nameplate. The brand owner selected the original symbol’s Ink blue, slate.750 (#175174), as OpenBoa’s main color.

**Design alternative to rule out.** A project food drawing or an agent face occupies the company signature slot. An approximation of the nine-dimple mark looks credible at poster size but its outline changes in the header.
