# I01 · Recognize who is signing a surface

Status: DRAFT, revision 2. Decision owner: OpenBoa design-system owner with the company identity maintainer. Source: [product intent, revision 1](../../../intent.md). [Paired specification](spec.md) · [Recognize the same company across surfaces](../../../journeys/J1/README.md).

## Need

An editor is placing the name of the company beside a project and an agent. A reader should recognize which entity speaks before reading the attribution.

## Success and counterexample

**Success.** Open a company header at its 160 px example width and a project detail at message size. A fresh reader can locate the company signature, the project mark and the named agent in their respective roles.

**Convincing failure.** A project food drawing or an agent face occupies the company signature slot. An approximation of the nine-dimple mark looks credible at poster size but its outline changes in the header.

## Limits

Use the selected Upright 9, B2 proportions and outlined 760 nameplate. The brand owner selected the original symbol’s Ink blue, slate.750 (#175174), as OpenBoa’s main color.
