# I21 design · Recognize an OpenBoa screen in everyday use

Parent: [originating intent, revision 2](intent.md). Review: NOT ACCEPTED. Design revision 2. [Recognize the same company across surfaces](../../../journeys/J1/README.md).

## Requirements

- **I21-U21A.** Use a paper or ink plane, soft geometry, a strong reading edge and Rethink Sans throughout. Let type, spacing and alignment carry hierarchy; reserve the richest color and the contour detail for the entity or decision that merits it.

**Constraint.** The brand owner owns art direction, foundation tokens and shared expression. Products own the facts, consequences and order implied by their tasks. The system-wide primary is the original symbol’s Ink blue, slate.750 (#175174).

**Design alternative to rule out.** Every surface has the same generic stack of rounded cards, lightly colored badges and a floating mascot. The layout is quiet but the decision, evidence and available action are impossible to rank.
